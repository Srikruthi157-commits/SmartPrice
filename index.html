Consumer Protection

Our Trust & Safety Principles

Honest pricing is our priority. Here is how we protect you from deceptive marketing tricks.

⚖️

We compare total cost, not just advertised prices.

Many websites display an artificially low base price, only to tack on steep shipping and handling fees at checkout. We calculate the complete amount.

📊

We show sample data clearly when live prices are unavailable.

When live network prices cannot be verified, we clearly label all numbers as demo data so you never mistake simulated numbers for live market contracts.

🔮

Our price predictions are estimates, not guarantees.

Smart purchase recommendations evaluate past trends to help you time purchases wisely, but commodity and retail prices may shift unexpectedly.

🛡️

Always verify the final price and seller details before purchasing.

We empower you with objective comparison data, but always confirm payment terms, return policies, and seller credibility on the final checkout portal.
