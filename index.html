🧠 How FraudShield Calculates Risk (For Hackathon & Demo)

FraudShield uses an intelligent client-side rule evaluation engine. It examines the message across 10 security vectors, computes weighted penalty scores, and classifies the message into standardized risk tiers:

🟢 Low Risk (0–29)

Likely Genuine: Normal conversational, informational, or transactional messages without aggressive threats, credential harvesting, or deceptive links.

🟡 Medium Risk (30–59)

Suspicious – Needs Verification: Contains warning flags such as unfamiliar links, generic courier notices, or vague financial claims that warrant careful verification.

🔴 High Risk (60–100)

Likely Fraud: Exhibits severe scam indicators such as account block threats, OTP/PIN requests, fake lotteries, high pressure, or malicious APK downloads.
